See your money clearly.

Cashlight is a personal finance app for Android. It tracks your income, bills, debts and savings, and always shows what's coming next — with everything kept on your own phone.

  • 📅 Automatic tracking. Pay days, bills and debt payments are recorded on their due dates, so balances stay up to date.
  • 💷 Safe to spend. How much you can spend before payday once bills, budgets and savings are set aside.
  • 🧾 Spending and statements. Log spending as it happens, or import a bank statement (CSV) and let Cashlight file it.
  • 🎯 Budgets, goals and debt payoff. Pace your spending, save towards goals and plan the fastest way out of debt.
  • 🔒 Private by design. No account, no servers, no adverts and no tracking. A PIN, fingerprint unlock and encrypted backups protect your data.

Backups to your own Google Drive

If you choose to, Cashlight can save a backup to your own Google Drive so you can restore your data on a new phone. Backups are encrypted with a password only you know before they leave your phone, and Cashlight can only see the backup files it creates — nothing else in your Drive. See the privacy policy for the details.
