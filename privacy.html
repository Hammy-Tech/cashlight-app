Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

In short: your financial information stays on your phone. Cashlight has no servers, no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and its developer never receives your data. If you turn on Google Drive backup, encrypted backups are saved to your own Google Drive, where only you can open them.

Who we are

Cashlight is a personal finance app for Android made by Hammy-Tech ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information you put into the app. You can contact us through the Cashlight app page on GitHub.

Information kept on your phone

Everything you enter — bank accounts and balances, income, bills, debts, spending, budgets, savings goals, imported bank statement rows and your settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Cashlight also keeps a few automatic snapshots of this data on your phone so it can be recovered after a mistake. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your PIN is never stored: only a secure, salted hash of it is kept, so it can be checked when you unlock the app. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Cashlight never sees your biometric data.

Bank statements you import (CSV files) are read on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications; no push service is involved.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, Cashlight asks you to sign in with Google and to allow access to Google Drive.

  • What it can access. Cashlight only requests the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage the files it creates itself — your Cashlight backups. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • What it stores there. Backup files of your Cashlight data, in a folder in your own Google Drive. Before a backup leaves your phone it is encrypted (AES-256) with a key made from a backup password that only you know. Without that password the backups can't be read — not by Google and not by us.
  • How it's used. Only to save your backups and, when you ask, to restore them onto your phone. Information from Google is not used for anything else, is not shared with anyone, is not used for advertising, and is never transferred to us.
  • Your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google on your phone. Cashlight doesn't receive your Google password, and it doesn't store your contacts, email or any other Google data.

Cashlight's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your backups in Google Drive are also covered by Google's privacy policy.

Backups you export yourself

You can also export a backup file and save or send it wherever you choose through Android's share menu. Once you share a file it is handled by the app you shared it with. You can protect exported backups with your backup password.

Information we collect

None. Cashlight contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information. This website does not use cookies or trackers either.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Uninstalling Cashlight, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.
  • Backups in Google Drive stay until you delete them from your Drive.
  • You can remove Cashlight's access to Google Drive at any time in your Google Account, under Third-party apps & services.

Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. You can lock the app with a PIN and fingerprint, hide it from screenshots and the recent apps screen, and encrypt backups with a password. No method is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and your backup password safe.

Children

Cashlight is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Changes will never reduce the protections described here for data you've already entered.
